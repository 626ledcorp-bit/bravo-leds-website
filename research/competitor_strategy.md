Competitor Strategy: Automotive LED Bulb Market
Research date: September 28, 2026
Purpose: practical teardown for BRAVO LEDS (website brand) / 626 LEDs (Rosemead store)

Covered: HID Kit Pros, PrecisionLED, LASFIT, SEALIGHT, AUXITO, Fahren, Beamtech, Diode Dynamics, Morimoto/The Retrofit Source, Alla Lighting, plus the SEO landscape (Amazon, affiliate roundups, HeadlightRevolution).

Dimensions per competitor: catalog structure, product listing format, product page anatomy, fitment finder UX, pricing/packaging strategy, marketing and trust, SEO.

================================================================
1. HID KIT PROS (hidkitpros.com)
================================================================

Catalog structure: WooCommerce. Interior packages live under /shop/{year-range}-{make}-{model}-led-lighting-package/. Also sells HID kits, audio, remote start, radar - a service shop that happens to sell online, not a pure e-commerce brand.

Product listing format: vehicle-specific interior packages priced "from $23.95" to "from $39.95" depending on vehicle. Example: 2015-2018 Tacoma package from $23.95; 2018-2019 Subaru XV Crosstrek from $27.95.

Product page anatomy: base kit plus optional add-on positions. Tacoma example: base = map x2 + dome x1; options add license plate x2, vanity mirror x4, reverse x2. Copy sections run Package Deal / Powerful / Options / Installation / Trusted. "Package savings vs individual bulbs" framing is explicit. Three white shades offered (warm, pure, cool) plus colors.

Fitment finder UX: "Search By Vehicle" on homepage, Fitment tab on product pages, and - importantly - trim exceptions are documented in copy (e.g. trucks without factory door lights). This kills returns.

Pricing/packaging: low displayed starting price (base kit) with optional add-ons raising the ticket. This is the core trick: $23.95 headline, $35-45 actual cart.

Marketing and trust: physical showroom and install center, trained staff, repeat customers and referrals, free non-marring pry tool in every package, free shipping over $199, phone number prominent. Positioning is "Quality Parts, Quality Service, Expected Results" - service-first, not price-first.

SEO: indexed per-vehicle package URLs rank for "[vehicle] led interior package" queries.

Steal: base-kit-plus-add-ons pricing, exact quantity/location tables with trim exceptions, free pry tool, package-vs-individual savings framing.

2. PRECISIONLED (precisionled.com)
================================================================

Catalog structure: massive long-tail catalog - the "Shop by Vehicle" index runs 365+ paginated pages. Two product lines per vehicle: Standard and Premium interior kits as separate SKUs. Also sells license plate packages ($14.99-$19.99), trunk strip lights ($17.99), headlight packages (Kepler Series, Premium CREE).

Product listing format: naming formula is "{Year} {Make} {Model} {Trim} {Product Type} ({years})" - e.g. "Audi S6 C6 LED License Plate Package (2006-2011)". Every listing shows sale price plus "Was" price. Category URLs follow /shop-by-vehicle/{make}/{model}-led.html.

Product page anatomy: standard interior kit sale $19.99 (was $30); premium $30.99. Two-tier choice is the whole interior-kit strategy: cheap standard hooks the price shopper, premium takes the margin.

Fitment finder UX: "Shop by Vehicle" make/model landing pages with model-specific SEO copy, product grids, sorting, and per-page controls. No complex YMM widget - the catalog IS the finder.

Pricing/packaging: everything on sale, always. 10% off code LVLUP10 observed. Standard vs premium split doubles the SKUs and captures both price points.

Marketing and trust: claims 5,000+ customer reviews and one of the industry's largest install video/guide libraries (200+ videos). YouTube install videos have chapter timestamps per location (map, vanity, dome, trunk, license plate, reverse) with standard and premium kit links in the description. Physical addresses prominent. help@precisionled.com "don't know your bulb size? just ask" - service-as-lead-gen.

SEO: this is the long-tail king. Hundreds of indexed "{year} {make} {model} LED" pages plus video SEO. Nobody else covers "[vehicle] interior LED kit" installs on YouTube at this scale.

Steal: standard/premium two-tier kits mapped to BRAVO tiers, vehicle landing pages that combine all products for one vehicle, install video per vehicle with kit links, "ask us your bulb size" email capture.

3. LASFIT (lasfit.com)
================================================================

Catalog structure: Shopify. Organized by bulb size collections (T10, 921, H11...) plus "Combo Package by Vehicle" and "Vehicle-Specific Custom LED" collections. Example: 2008-2012 VW CC full-vehicle combo at $240.35.

Product listing format: size-first, not vehicle-first. Product titles lead with fit sizes: "T10 194 168 CANBUS Error Free LED License Plate Dome Map Trunk Cargo Light".

Product page anatomy - the best in the business for conversion:
- "Select Your Vehicle - Confirm fitment before choosing options" sits ABOVE the variant selectors. Fitment is step one, not an afterthought.
- "Installation Location" variant picker: Back up Light (1 pair) / Cargo Light (1 pair) - the customer buys by position, not by SKU.
- Quantity ladders: 1 pair $9.99 / 2 pairs $15.99 / 3 pairs $20.99 / 5 pairs $26.99. Bulk pricing built into the variant selector.
- Spec block: Fit Size (exhaustive alias list), Installation Position, Lumens, Wattage, Color.
- "5K+ bought in past month" social proof badges.
- Promo: 15% off code with live countdown timer (HAR2615 observed).
- Gift-with-purchase tiers: spend $120 get free L-T10 bulbs, spend $200 get free L-T10 + L-T15.
- Honest fitment warnings per product (e.g. "these CANBUS bulbs don't work for reverse on 2021-2023 Tahoe..." with a vehicle list).

Fitment finder UX: fitment-first as described; also custom-fit SKUs for problem vehicles (Pro-VW-01 H7 with retainer for Passat, with install caveats documented honestly).

Pricing/packaging: tiered series - LC Plus H11 $49.99 sale, Pro series up to $128.99. Free US shipping over $99. 1-year hassle-free warranty, 45-day free returns (90 days for Lasfit club members). Lasfit club = membership with extended returns.

Marketing and trust: US-based support, lifetime installation support, bought-this-month badges, countdown promos.

SEO: size-based product pages rank for "[size] LED bulb" queries; combo-by-vehicle pages capture "[vehicle] LED package".

Steal: fitment-first page layout, position-based variant selectors, quantity ladders with bulk pricing, gift-with-purchase tiers, countdown promo codes, bought-this-month badges, honest per-vehicle incompatibility lists.

4. SEALIGHT (sealight.com + Amazon)
================================================================

Catalog structure: Amazon-first brand; sealight.com exists but Amazon is the real storefront. This is the template for winning on marketplaces rather than DTC.

Product listing format: Amazon listings with kitchen-sink titles: "SEALIGHT 2026 Upgraded H11 9005 LED Bulbs Combo, 1:1 Design 100,000LM 900% Brighter..."

Product page anatomy: lumen arms race - 50,000 to 100,000LM claims per pair/combo, "+900% brighter", 6500K cool white, 1:1 mini halogen-size design, built-in CANbus decoder, "99% vehicle compatibility", 3-minute install, 70,000-hour lifespan claims.

Pricing/packaging: 4-pack combos (H11+9005) around $63-84 CAD observed; aggressive value bundling is the whole pitch.

Marketing and trust: wins "best LED headlight bulbs" affiliate roundups (madtasting, strongmocha) which are really Amazon affiliate funnels. Review volume on Amazon is the trust layer.

SEO: doesn't do SEO - rides Amazon's domain authority and affiliate listicles.

Steal: combo-pack bundling (low+high beam 4-packs). Do NOT copy the inflated lumen claims - see Diode Dynamics note below; BRAVO can differentiate on honesty instead.

5. AUXITO (auxito.com)
================================================================

Catalog structure: Shopify. "For Vehicle" collection holds vehicle combo pages like "2015-2022 GMC Canyon LED Bulbs" ($84.88) alongside size-based products.

Product listing format: vehicle combo pages bundle the full-vehicle recommendation: per-position spec breakdown (high beam 9005, low beam H11, fog 5202, turn 7443, backup 921, license T10...) plus a "Package Included" list. This is the single best-executed "everything for your vehicle" page in the market.

Product page anatomy: "Vehicle Check - Select your vehicle to see verified fitment" on every product page. Tab structure: Detail / (specs) / Review. Embedded YouTube videos. Spec tables with lumens, wattage, color temp, IP rating. Color variants: 3000K golden yellow and 8000K ice blue fog options. Switchback turn signals as a differentiator product.

Fitment finder UX: vehicle check widget per product plus dedicated vehicle combo pages.

Pricing/packaging: pairs $40-80, vehicle combos $46-85. 2-year warranty.

Marketing and trust: 2-year warranty (beats Lasfit's 1-year), #AUXITOLED community UGC hashtag, embedded install videos, "600% brighter than halogen" claims.

SEO: vehicle combo pages capture "[vehicle] LED bulbs" queries.

Steal: the vehicle combo page format (per-position table + package-included list + one price) is the single highest-value page template to copy for BRAVO.

6. FAHREN (Amazon-native)
================================================================

No meaningful DTC site; sells through Amazon with Fulfillment. Pairs $45-63, combos similar. 40,000-80,000LM claims, 18,000 RPM turbo fans, TIR optics, 360-degree adjustable collar rings, built-in CANbus. Lives off Amazon's "Frequently bought together" cross-sell. Lesson: Amazon-native brands compete purely on spec claims and price - BRAVO can't and shouldn't fight them there; the edge is fitment accuracy, install help, and local presence.

7. BEAMTECH (beamtechs.com + Amazon)
================================================================

Catalog structure: simple Shopify DTC plus Amazon. One product per bulb size - the simplest catalog in the set.

Product page anatomy: urgency widgets everywhere - "2 sold in last 8 hours", "283 customers are viewing this product". Sale strikethroughs ($50 down to $35.99). Tabs: Description / Customer Reviews. Fanless positioning (no noise, better radio signal).

Pricing/packaging: $36-46 per pair on sale. 3-year warranty plus lifetime support.

Marketing and trust: email popup capture ("You have successfully subscribed"), 3-year warranty as differentiator at this price.

SEO: minimal; Amazon carries them.

Steal: urgency widgets (sold-recently, viewers-now) are cheap to implement with real data; 3-year warranty at value pricing; email capture popup with discount.

8. DIODE DYNAMICS (diodedynamics.com)
================================================================

The premium engineering brand. Key differentiator: "All Diode Dynamics LED lighting is rated with accurate, measured lumen figures, not calculations or guesses. We test the true output in-house." This is a direct attack on the SEALIGHT/Fahren lumen arms race - and it works for buyers who've been burned.

Catalog structure: good-better-best tiers per size - HP48 ($24.95 single) / HP11 / XP80 ($49.95 single), each step up in output with honest lumen numbers (250LM vs 510LM).

Product page anatomy: fixed four-section copy template - Upgrade / Brightness / Reliability / Experience. Every product follows it. Extremely consistent.

Fitment finder UX: standard; strength is dealer network (AutoZone, Fitment Industries, SD Wheel) more than the finder.

Pricing/packaging: premium singles, not pairs. 3-year replacement warranty ("industry-leading").

Marketing and trust: engineered and assembled in the USA, decade-plus in business, dealer network, honest specs.

Compliance note: their AutoZone listing states "NOTE: LED bulbs are legal in the USA for Fog Light use only. Not compliant with DOT / FMVSS108 and not street legal in the USA for Headlights." This matches BRAVO's settled compliance stance exactly.

Steal: measured-vs-claimed lumens as a trust differentiator ("we publish real numbers, not 100,000LM fantasy"), the four-section copy template, 3-year warranty framing, DOT disclaimer language.

9. MORIMOTO / THE RETROFIT SOURCE (theretrofitsource.com)
================================================================

The enthusiast-premium tier. Morimoto 2Stroke 4.0 at $258-278 per pair with a Lifetime Warranty. TRS is a multi-brand marketplace (Morimoto, GTR Lighting, XenonDepot, SV4) with faceted filtering by bulb size.

Price ladder per size: CMB $47 (1-year) / Xtreme LED Pro $169 (2-year) / S-V.4 $150-190 (2-year) / 2Stroke 4.0 $258 (lifetime) / GTR Ultra 3.0 $259-289 (lifetime). Yellow fog variants as separate SKUs.

"OUR PICK" badges mark house favorites. Sale section with strikethroughs ("PRICE DROP!").

SEO: HeadlightRevolution (headlightrevolution.com, a retailer) owns the enthusiast lane with its "Best LED Bulb of 2026" blog: "lumen claims mean very little without beam control... The bulbs that stand out share proper emitter placement, controlled hotspot, defined cutoff." Ranks GTR Ultra 3 #1, Morimoto 2Stroke 4.0 as premium pick. Content + commerce flywheel.

Steal: "OUR PICK" badges, yellow fog as separate SKUs, the content-commerce flywheel (blog/video reviews that rank and sell). BRAVO's install videos can serve the same function at local scale.

10. ALLA LIGHTING (allalighting.com)
================================================================

SoCal company (909 area code, Inland Empire - near enough to be a local competitor). This is the closest business model to BRAVO: independent, phone-first, service-heavy.

Catalog structure: size-based products with item numbers (Item #: 16897369).

Product page anatomy: "Enter Vehicle To Check Fit" YMM widget on every product page. "Product's Review" and "Installation Guide" tabs on every product. "Have a Question? Contact us!! 909 606 2935" - phone above the fold.

Fitment finder UX: YMM widget plus an HONEST fitment disclaimer: "Bulb size guide is only for reference. May not be 100% accurate... trim packages, manufacturer and aftermarket modifications [vary]. To confirm, please remove the bulb and compare the socket." This honesty reduces disputes.

Pricing/packaging: deep discount presentation ($139.99 down to $79.99, $99.99 to $49.99). Free domestic shipping. "Lowest Prices, Best Value, In-House Expert Support / Guaranteed Fitment."

Compliance - the best language in the market, on every product: "Not compliant with DOT / FMVSS108 and not street legal in the USA for forward lightings. Legal for off-road use in ATV, snowmobile, and off-road racing use, only auxiliary for forward lighting... Note: This usage regulation is not unique to Alla Lighting LED bulbs. All LED bulbs from every brand, regardless of marketing claims, are prohibited from street use in LED forward lightings in the USA." BRAVO should adopt this framing nearly verbatim (adapted to house style, no "headlight" word).

Steal: phone-first product pages, per-product install guide tabs, honest fitment disclaimer, the DOT disclaimer language, free-shipping threshold.

11. SEO LANDSCAPE
================================================================

- "Best LED headlight/bulb" queries: owned by Amazon affiliate roundups (madtasting, strongmocha) featuring SEALIGHT, Fahren, Auxito, Auxbeam, Termitor, Firehawk. AI-generated listicles. Nobody independent ranks except via affiliates.
- YouTube "top 5" videos: Amazon affiliate funnels (Fahren, SEALIGHT, Auxito links in descriptions).
- Enthusiast lane: HeadlightRevolution blog (retailer-owned content).
- Long-tail "[year] [make] [model] LED": PrecisionLED's 365-page catalog, AUXITO vehicle pages, LASFIT combo-by-vehicle pages. This is the winnable lane for BRAVO - none of these target "[vehicle] LED bulbs Rosemead / Los Angeles" or install-intent queries.
- "[Vehicle] interior LED kit": PrecisionLED owns video; HID Kit Pros owns product pages. Thin competition - BRAVO's 21 interior kits can rank here with install videos.
- Amazon owns "[size] LED bulb" transactional queries. Don't fight that; win on fitment + install + local.

12. CROSS-CUTTING PATTERNS (what everyone does)
================================================================

1. Fitment-first: Lasfit puts vehicle selection above variant selection; Auxito has "Vehicle Check" on every page; Alla has "Enter Vehicle To Check Fit". Nobody makes you pick a size blind.
2. Position-based buying: Lasfit's "Installation Location" picker, HID Kit Pros' add-on positions. Customers think in positions, not SKUs.
3. Quantity ladders: Lasfit's 1/2/3/5-pair pricing on miniature bulbs.
4. Always-on-sale: PrecisionLED, Beamtech, Alla - strikethrough "Was" pricing on everything. (Use selectively at BRAVO; constant fake discounts erode trust.)
5. Urgency: countdown timers (Lasfit), sold-recently/viewers-now (Beamtech), bought-this-month badges (Lasfit).
6. Video: PrecisionLED 200+ install videos, Auxito embedded videos, Alla install-guide tabs. Video is table stakes.
7. Reviews as trust layer: star counts on every product page, review counts in category grids.
8. Warranty ladder as positioning: 1-yr (Lasfit, BRAVO) / 2-yr (Auxito) / 3-yr (Diode Dynamics, Beamtech) / lifetime (Morimoto). BRAVO's 1-year on everything is competitive at its price.
9. Gift/promo mechanics: Lasfit gift tiers, Beamtech email popup, PrecisionLED 10% code.
10. Compliance: Alla and Diode Dynamics both run explicit DOT/FMVSS108 disclaimers. The honest players disclose; the Amazon brands don't.

BRAVO's price position: Basic $25 / Plus $35 / Premium $70 / Platinum $100 per pair. This undercuts Lasfit and Auxito DTC pricing, matches Beamtech sale pricing and Amazon brands, and sits far below Morimoto. The tiers already map to the market's good-better-best structure - the site just needs to present them that way.

13. RECOMMENDATIONS FOR BRAVO LEDS (prioritized)
================================================================
Rules: small owner-operator only. No enterprise fluff. Each item is buildable by one person with the existing site.

HIGH IMPACT
-----------

1. Vehicle landing pages: one indexed page per year/make/model that lists every position (forward, fog, turn, brake, reverse, interior) with bulb size and matching BRAVO products. Copy the AUXITO "2015-2022 GMC Canyon LED Bulbs" format: per-position spec table plus "package included" bundle price. This is the single best SEO and upsell engine in the market and BRAVO's fitment data already supports it. Effort: medium (template + data already in DB).

2. Fitment-first product pages. Move vehicle/position selection above the buy button, Lasfit-style: "Select your vehicle - confirm fitment before choosing options." On miniature bulbs (T10/194, 921) add an Installation Location picker (map/dome/license/trunk) and quantity ladders (1 pair / 2 pairs / 3 pairs with bulk pricing). This raises average ticket with zero new products. Effort: low-medium.

3. Exact "what's included" tables on all 21 interior kits: position, bulb size, quantity, location, plus trim/sunroof exceptions (copy HID Kit Pros' honesty here). Add a free pry tool to every interior kit and "package price vs buying bulbs individually" savings framing. This kills the #1 pre-purchase question and the #1 return reason. Effort: low.

4. One install video per top-20 vehicle, filmed during real store installs, posted to YouTube with kit links and timestamps per location (PrecisionLED's exact playbook). An owner-operator who installs daily can out-produce every competitor here. Videos rank for "[vehicle] interior LED install" and sell while you sleep. Effort: medium, ongoing.

5. Trust and urgency layer on every page: real star counts and review counts on products and category grids, "X bought this month" badges from real order data, and one standing promo code with a real expiry (Lasfit/Beamtech pattern). BRAVO has the physical store and real customers - show it. Effort: low.

MEDIUM IMPACT
-------------

6. Gift-with-purchase tiers: spend $X get free T10 bulbs, spend $Y get T10+T15 (Lasfit pattern). Moves slow miniature-bulb stock and lifts order value. Effort: low.

7. Yellow (3000K) and ice-blue (8000K) fog variants as separate SKUs/options (AUXITO/Morimoto pattern). Zero tooling, pure margin. Effort: low.

8. "Enter vehicle to check fit" widget on every product page plus Alla Lighting's honest fitment disclaimer ("guide for reference; trim packages vary; compare your socket"). Reduces disputes and chargebacks. Effort: low.

9. Combo packs: low+high beam 4-packs and full-vehicle bundles at a bundle price (Lasfit "Combo Package by Vehicle", Auxito vehicle pages). Effort: low.

10. Email/SMS capture: exit-intent or timed popup offering a discount code (Beamtech pattern), plus PrecisionLED's "don't know your bulb size? email us" lead-gen on the finder. Effort: low.

11. Honest-lumens positioning: publish real measured lumens and say so - "we publish measured output, not 100,000LM fantasy numbers" (Diode Dynamics pattern). Differentiates against Amazon brands without a price war. Effort: low.

12. Structured product copy template on every product: Upgrade / Brightness / Reliability / Experience (Diode Dynamics pattern). Makes the catalog feel professional with one template. Effort: low-medium.

LOW IMPACT (do later)
---------------------

13. "OUR PICK" badges on best sellers (TRS pattern). Effort: trivial.
14. Membership/club with extended returns (Lasfit club: 90-day returns for members). Effort: medium; only after volume justifies it.
15. Dealer/wholesale program (Diode Dynamics model). Out of scope until DTC is solid.

COMPLIANCE NOTE
---------------
Adopt Alla Lighting's DOT disclaimer framing nearly verbatim, adapted to house style (no "headlight" word): not compliant with DOT/FMVSS108, not street legal in the USA for forward lighting; legal for off-road/fog/auxiliary use; note that this applies to every brand's LED bulbs regardless of marketing claims. Put it on every forward-lighting product page and the existing /dot-compliance page. This is both legally safer and a trust signal.

4. Film one install video per top-20 vehicle during real store installs and post to YouTube with kit links, because PrecisionLED's 200-video library is their moat and an owner-operator who installs daily can replicate it cheaply.
