Drop your SEMA Data export files here on day one:

  aces_*.xml          ACES application data (BaseVehicleID -> part number).
                      This is the primary input.
  vcdb_vehicles.csv   BaseVehicleID -> year, make, model, trim mapping
                      (from your VCdb subscription export, or the vehicle
                      reference bundled in your SEMA Data download).
  sema_fitment_*.csv  Optional flat-file fallback. Columns:
                      vcdb_vehicle_id, year, make, model, trim,
                      position, part_number, note

Then run:  cd ~/workspace/626leds/website && python3 sema_import.py

Full instructions: website/README.md ("SEMA Data" section).
Sample synthetic files for pipeline testing only: ../sample_synthetic/
