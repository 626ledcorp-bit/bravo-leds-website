# Batch 4 hero image style spec (owner-confirmed 2026-10-04)

Photorealistic night shot of the vehicle, three-quarter front view, on wet
asphalt with rain and city-light reflections (match existing heroes like
hero-toyota-tacoma-2016.jpg).

REQUIRED lighting (owner explicit):
- Headlights ON with bright white LED headlight bulbs (crisp 6000k white,
  clearly glowing, lens flare acceptable)
- Fog lights ON with white LED foglight bulbs (bright white, not yellow)

Do not render yellow/amber fogs. Do not render headlights off or dim.
After generation: run watermark_heroes.py on the new files (BRAVO LEDS,
bottom-right, light gray caps) before deploy.
