Before you start any install

  • Work with the engine off and the lights switched off. If your owner's manual says so — or you're ever unsure — disconnect the negative battery terminal first.
  • Wear gloves or use a clean cloth. Bare fingers leave oils on LED chips and lenses that create hot spots and shorten bulb life.
  • LEDs are polarity-sensitive: if a new bulb doesn't light, pull it and flip it 180°.
  • Flicker, strobe, or a "bulb out" dash warning after install usually means your car wants a CANbus decoder.
