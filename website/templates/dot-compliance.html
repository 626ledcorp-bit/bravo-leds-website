DOT Rules & Proper Use

⚠️ For off-road and fog light use only. Not DOT/SAE approved for on-road use. Check your local laws.

What DOT/SAE approval means

Vehicle headlight assemblies are certified as a complete unit under Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108 (FMVSS 108). The certification covers the housing, reflector, lens, and the exact light source it was designed around.

Swapping a different bulb technology — like an LED bulb — into a housing designed for halogen changes the beam pattern the assembly was certified with. Because of that, retrofit LED bulbs in halogen housings are not DOT/SAE approved for on-road headlight use, regardless of brand.

Proper use for our LED bulbs

  • Off-road vehicles and trails — rigs that don't run on public roads.
  • Auxiliary off-road lighting — pods, bars, and supplemental lights used off public roadways.
  • Fog light applications — where permitted; fog lamps sit under separate rules from headlamps in many jurisdictions.

Always check your state and local laws before installing. Rules vary, and enforcement is up to local authorities — when in doubt, keep them off the street.
